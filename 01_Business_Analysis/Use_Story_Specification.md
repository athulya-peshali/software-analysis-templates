## 5. INVEST Checklist

- [ ] **Independent:** Can be developed and delivered on its own.
- [ ] **Negotiable:** Leaves room for technical discussion.
- [ ] **Valuable:** Delivers clear business or user value.
- [ ] **Estimable:** Clear enough for the team to size.
- [ ] **Small:** Can be completed within a single sprint.
- [ ] **Testable:** Acceptance criteria are clear and verifiable.
