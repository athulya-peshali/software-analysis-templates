## 2. Role Definitions

* **(R) Responsible:** The person(s) who does the actual work to complete the deliverable.
* **(A) Accountable:** The single individual with final approval authority and ownership. *(Strict Rule: Only ONE 'A' per task).*
* **(C) Consulted:** Subject matter experts providing necessary input before or during the work.
* **(I) Informed:** Stakeholders kept up-to-date on progress or completion without contributing directly.

## 4. Governance Checklist

- [ ] Every task has at least one **Responsible (R)**.
- [ ] Every task has **exactly one Accountable (A)**.
- [ ] No single role is overloaded with too many **R**s.
- [ ] Communication overhead is managed (not everyone needs to be **C** or **I**).
